Closeout

Close the product against its intended outcome

A closeout is not a deletion checklist. It is an evidence-gated decision record that explains why work will continue, narrow, pause, archive, or stop.

The six decisions captured by the Project Closeout record
Record groupWhat it must establishFailure it prevents
Decision identityProject, exact snapshot, date, authority, disposition, and any external action.An ambiguous stop that no accountable person actually made.
Outcome testEvidence for and against the intended result, missing evidence, and whether another bounded test is justified.Using effort, feature count, or absence of catastrophe as continuation evidence.
Evidence classesVerified facts, inferences, assumptions, unknowns, and Owner decisions remain separate.A project judgment being rewritten as a technical fact.
Retained materialReproducible artifacts, tests, decisions, reusable controls, and known limitations.Discarding useful learning together with the product hypothesis.
Non-transferUnsupported product, production, compliance, independence, or universal-method claims stay behind.Moving a failed or untested claim into a different project.
Reopening and handoffNew evidence, authority, next-test budget, immediate stops, retained locations, and prohibited claims.A standing promise quietly reactivating the old mandate.
Valid outcome

A closeout may produce no method change. Preserving a well-supported negative result is more useful than promoting a ceremonial rule.

Re-test

Test a possible lesson before making it a rule

The Method-Change Re-test compares one candidate rule with the current method. The candidate earns promotion only if it changes a material result with proportionate burden.

  1. 01Freeze the candidate. Name the proposed rule, source learning, current baseline, expected material error, scope, and authorized evaluator.
  2. 02Freeze one observable difference. Record what the candidate changes and the extra time, tokens, artifacts, or review burden it introduces.
  3. 03Declare the decision rule. Support only if the candidate changes a material decision or catches a material error earlier than the baseline.
  4. 04Protect authority. The Builder cannot be the sole judge of materiality. A validated result still requires an Owner authorization reference and explicit scope before the rule becomes active.
  5. 05Record the result afterward. Preserve the frozen reference, baseline and candidate results, burden, counterexamples, challenge, and Owner decision.
Fail closed

If the baseline, event, evaluator, evidence, or acceptance authority changes, stop the comparison. Missing evidence produces unknown, not support.

Case

A product was closed. A smaller practice survived.

The experimental AI-Native Systems Harness tested whether contracts, governed roles, evidence rules and gate records, controlled learning, token budgets, adapters, and reports should become one standalone open-source product.

Engineering fact

The project produced a frozen runnable prototype and bounded internal verification.

What remained unknown

There was no evidence of external adoption, unaided use, production effectiveness, or willingness to accept the additional workflow friction.

Owner decision

The standalone product track was archived instead of treating completed engineering as product validation.

What the closeout preserved

The reproducible prototype and evidence history remained inspectable. Unsupported roadmap and product claims did not transfer.

Transfer result

The first prospective comparison found no additive prevention beyond the existing GDAC baseline.

What survived

A bounded Outcome Contract, an evidence-gated Project Closeout, and a prospective Method-Change Re-test survived as smaller practice tools.

Rule

The decision rule to reuse

Close, preserve, test

Stop the product when its product evidence fails the continuation gate. Preserve only the artifacts and learning that survive a separate evidence test. Promote no cross-project rule without a recorded baseline comparison, preventive value, and the required authority.

This case does not establish that the product had no possible users, that its controls were ineffective, or that the retained practices improve real-world outcomes. It demonstrates a governed way to preserve those unknowns while still making a stop decision.
