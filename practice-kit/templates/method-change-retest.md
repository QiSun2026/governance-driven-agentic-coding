## 5. Falsification conditions

Narrow, hold, or retire the candidate if:

- it changes no permitted state or useful detection timing;
- the baseline already blocks the same material error;
- it creates ceremonial fields without decision value;
- false-positive redo burden exceeds the prevented harm;
- the producer becomes the sole judge of materiality or risk tier; or
- the result depends on evidence selected after the outcome was known.

## 6. Predeclared verdicts

- `supported_additive`: prevents or materially advances detection of an error
  not explicitly stopped by the recorded baseline, with proportionate burden.
- `supported_clarification`: makes the boundary clearer or more enforceable,
  but the baseline already stops the error.
- `no_additive_value`: changes no permitted state or useful timing.
- `modify`: a narrower rule helps but the candidate is overbroad.
- `disconfirmed`: produces incorrect or disproportionate blocking.
- `unknown`: contemporaneous evidence is insufficient; unknown is not a pass.
