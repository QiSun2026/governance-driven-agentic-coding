## 2. Outcome test

- Intended accepted outcome:
- Evidence that the outcome was achieved:
- Evidence that the outcome was not achieved:
- Evidence still missing:
- Does the remaining opportunity justify another bounded test? Why?

Do not use effort already spent, test count, feature count, or absence of a
catastrophic failure as evidence that the project should continue.

## 6. Method-learning candidates

For each possible lesson:

- Candidate statement:
- Existing method baseline:
- Claimed additive value:
- Source evidence:
- Counterexamples:
- Preventive check:
- Applicability boundary:
- State: `candidate | validated | active | retired`
- Validation evidence and expiry (required for `validated`):
- Owner authorization reference and active scope (required for `active`):

`validated` means the declared prospective comparison met its rule and retained
its evidence, burden, counterexamples, limits, and expiry. It does not activate
the candidate. No Agent may self-authorize `active` or rewrite global rules.

A closeout may produce no method change. That is a valid result.
