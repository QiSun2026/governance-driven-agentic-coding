Workflow

The smallest useful workflow

Do not begin by filling every template. Choose one important, reversible, testable task and use only the controls that change how it can be accepted.

  1. 01Choose the task. It should matter enough to govern, but remain small enough to reverse and test.
  2. 02Freeze the Outcome Contract. State the result, non-goals, write paths, whole-task budget, stop rules, and required evidence.
  3. 03Freeze the Eval Plan before a candidate exists. Define the subject-selection rule, claims, graders, evidence, separation, and retry policy.
  4. 04Validate both records together. Catch risk, claim, grader, evidence, role, or authority drift before implementation.
  5. 05Build within the declared boundary. Every attempt, failure, and review consumes the same whole-task budget.
  6. 06Bind and evaluate one exact candidate. Required results attach to the immutable post-build reference, not an unversioned description.
  7. 07Validate the Gate Record. The exact candidate, evidence digests, results, contradictions, findings, and budget usage derive one blocked or ready Harness Gate.
  8. 08Record the Owner disposition. Accept, set conditions, request rework, pause, or stop. The technical gate does not make this decision.

Boundary

Keep the boundary clear

  • aFacts, inferences, assumptions, unknowns, and Owner decisions stay separate.
  • bMissing evidence does not become a pass, zero, approval, or readiness.
  • cThe pre-build Eval Plan does not contain the post-build candidate reference.
  • dTechnical eval results, the aggregate Harness Gate, and Owner disposition remain separate.
  • eThe Builder cannot be the sole grader of its material non-deterministic claim.
  • fBuilder attempts, evaluator reruns, and stochastic trials use different records and budgets.
  • gA project may propose a cross-project rule but cannot activate it by itself.

Provenance

Why these files are here

The kit includes controls extracted from the closeout of the experimental AI-Native Systems Harness, then reconciled with the fuller authority, record, review, and acceptance architecture of GDAC v1.3.

The standalone runtime, adapters, dashboards, synthetic pass rates, and product hypothesis remain closed. The retained result is a runtime-independent Harness architecture, Eval rule set, and machine-checkable pre-work and post-build record chain.

Integrity

The current GDAC v2.0 release is governed by the repository-root checksum manifest. The previous v1.5 release remains available at commit 39ff3cd, and the Practice Kit has its own directory-level manifest. Matching checksums establish file identity, not correctness or effectiveness.
