Continuity · v1.3 to GDAC v2.0

What changed, and what stayed

v2.0 builds on v1.3 rather than replacing it: the control spine stays, evaluation becomes machine-checkable, and the AI-company metaphor becomes portable Harness functions.

Retained

Retained: the control spine

The names change where the old metaphor obscured the mechanism. The underlying controls have a direct home in the GDAC v2.0 Harness and Eval method.

Added

Added: an explicit evaluation protocol

v1.3 described review and acceptance at a high level. v2.0 specifies how a frozen claim and material risk become evidence, one aggregate gate result, and a separate Owner decision.

Before implementation
An Eval Plan is frozen to the canonical Contract digest. Each material claim or risk names its grader, evidence, pass rule, conflict rule, rerun and trial budget, and failure consequence.
One traceable chain
Every material criterion follows claim -> eval -> grader -> evidence -> gate. Owner acceptance remains a separate disposition, not another Eval.
One exact candidate
Results bind to a Candidate Binding with artifact digest, source revision, and attempt. A changed candidate or evaluation rule invalidates affected results and requires a new binding.
Post-build closure
A Gate Record retains failed attempts, evidence references and digests, eval states, contradictions, findings, the derived blocked or ready state, and the separate Owner disposition.
Explicit result states
Evaluations record pass, fail, insufficient_evidence, or not_evaluated. Required failures and evidence gaps keep the Harness Gate blocked.
Risk-matched graders
Deterministic, regression, adversarial, security, model, and qualified-human checks are selected by the claim and material risk, not by test volume.
Controlled attempts
Build attempts, evaluator reruns, and stochastic trials keep separate records and budgets. A later pass does not erase an earlier failure or known limitation.
Machine-checkable record chain
The repository supplies schemas, validators, and a worked chain for the Outcome Contract, Eval Plan, Candidate Binding, evidence, Gate Result, and Owner disposition.

Reshaped

Reshaped: functions without standing offices

The useful functions remain. They now appear as temporary responsibilities and read-only views instead of a permanent AI organization.

Investor Office
Becomes an Owner read-only view of current state, material change, risk, blockers, and decisions required.
Academy
Becomes a controlled learning view and prospective re-test. A project may propose a lesson, but evidence and Owner authority control whether it becomes active.
GTM and Narrative
Becomes an evidence-backed public view. Publication remains a separate Owner decision, and the view cannot rewrite the underlying record.
Managing Director
Becomes a bounded set of Orchestrator, Builder, and Integrator responsibilities rather than a permanent executive Agent.
Independent challenge
Is described as context separation unless the reviewer is a genuinely separate party with suitable authority and competence.

A role exists only when it brings new evidence, necessary expertise, a materially different challenge, or a control that changes authority, state, or a decision.

Removed

Not carried forward: metaphor and unsupported scope

Not every part of the earlier presentation belongs in an executable method. GDAC v2.0 leaves out three categories.

Organization shape

No mandatory company chart.

No standing AI departments

The method does not require an AI-company organization, a fixed number of red teams, or permanent offices. Responsibilities appear only when the task needs them.

Outcome claims

No automatic compounding story.

No unmeasured benefit claims

A project does not automatically create Owner capability, market assets, adoption, lower cost, or better delivery. Uninstrumented organizational KPIs are not retained.

Product boundary

No hidden runtime expansion.

No full Agent platform

GDAC v2.0 does not provide an Agent runner, scheduler, persistent evidence database, execution sandbox, deployment system, Owner identity service, or compliance certification layer.

No self-authorizing learning

A lesson or candidate rule cannot activate itself, expand to other projects, or rewrite the Harness without the required evidence and Owner authority.

The resulting method

Define the outcome and authority. Lock the evidence rules. Let Agents work inside the boundary. Challenge the exact result through a separate path. Give the full record to the Owner for the final decision.
